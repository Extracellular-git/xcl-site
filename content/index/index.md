# About Extracellular

Extracellular is the partner of choice for accelerating sustainable biotech. We combine deep bioprocess expertise with cutting-edge technical solutions to help our partners develop efficient, scalable, and sustainable bioprocesses.

## Our Mission

At Extracellular, we're committed to transforming the biotech industry by making bioprocesses more:

- **Sustainable** - Creating processes that can run efficiently over extended periods
- **Scalable** - Developing high-yield systems even from small reactors
- **Efficient** - Implementing automation to reduce waste and optimize resources

## Technical Capabilities

Extracellular brings together bioprocess engineering expertise with modern technical solutions:

### Custom Control Systems

We develop cost-effective, flexible control systems that integrate disparate tools and equipment:

- Modular controller architecture for seamless integration of various inputs and outputs
- Digital communication with equipment using open standards (Modbus RTU, RS485)
- Custom hardware solutions at a fraction of the cost of vendor-specific systems

### Process Optimization

Our team specializes in optimizing bioprocesses for maximum efficiency:

- Continuous bioprocessing with real-time measurement and feedback
- PID control systems for maintaining optimal growth conditions
- Data-driven decision making for process improvement

### Technical Infrastructure

We build robust technical infrastructure to support bioprocessing:

- Reliable control systems running on modern hardware
- Remote monitoring and control capabilities
- Integration of sensors and actuators from multiple vendors
